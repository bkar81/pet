What PET is

PET (Personal Expense Tracker) is a free, open-source personal expense tracking tool. It runs entirely in your browser — there is no company, account, or service behind it beyond the code itself.

Your data

Your transactions, categories, and account data are stored locally in your browser's storage on your own device. Nobody else can access this data — it never passes through any server operated by the developer.

If you choose to connect Google Drive sync, your data is copied only to your own Google Drive account, using access that is limited to files PET itself creates. This is entirely optional and off by default. See the Privacy Policy for details.

No warranty

PET is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. The developer makes no guarantees about accuracy, availability, or fitness for any particular purpose. You are responsible for keeping your own backups (PET's Export feature makes this easy) and for verifying your own financial records independently.

Acceptable use

PET is intended for personal, individual expense tracking. Please don't use it to store data unlawfully, or attempt to interfere with the Google APIs it connects to beyond normal use of the sync feature.

Open source

PET's source code is publicly available and licensed under the MIT License. You're free to inspect it, fork it, modify it, and run your own copy. Each copy operates independently with its own separate data.
