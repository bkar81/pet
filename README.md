# PET has moved

PET (Personal Expense Tracker) is now **PIE Tracker — Personal Income Expense Tracker**.

- App: https://bkar81.github.io/pie/
- Code: https://github.com/bkar81/pie

Old PET links forward to PIE Tracker automatically. Your data stays on your device: open PIE Tracker on the same device and it picks up your PET data. You can also restore a PET backup in PIE Tracker under Settings → Data.
