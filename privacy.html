Privacy Policy for PET App

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Storage

PET App uses Google OAuth solely to connect to your personal Google Drive account. The application stores your application data and settings directly within your own Google Drive storage space.

2. Data Collection and Usage

PET App does not own, operate, or maintain any external database or server to store, transmit, or process your personal information. All app data and preferences remain strictly within your personal Google Account.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not collect, share, track, or sell any user data or personal information to third parties.

4. Access Revocation

You can revoke PET App's access to your Google Drive account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions Settings.
